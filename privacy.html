Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains what information ChimeRaid Ecom (“we”, “us”) handles when you visit chimeraid.com or get in touch with us about our Shopify dropshipping mentorship.

1. What we collect

  • What you send us. When you apply or message us through Linktree, WhatsApp or Discord, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, store details and goals. Those platforms have their own privacy policies.
  • Technical data. This website has no accounts and no forms. Like any website, the services that deliver it (our hosting provider, and the services that supply our fonts, icons, styles and some images) can see your IP address, browser type and the pages you request.
  • Your cookie choice. We save whether you accepted or declined the cookie notice in your browser’s local storage so we don’t keep asking. It stays on your device.

We don’t currently run advertising pixels or analytics trackers on this website. If that changes, we will update this page and the cookie notice first.

2. How we use information

  • To reply to applications and questions.
  • To run the mentorship and support students.
  • To keep the site working and secure.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Student results, messages and videos

This site shows student dashboards, messages and video reviews. If you appear on the site and want something changed or taken down, contact us and we will remove it.

4. Sharing

We don’t sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers we use to communicate and host the site, and when the law requires it. Some of these providers process data in other countries.

5. How long we keep it

We keep messages and application details for as long as we need them for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise them. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU or UK GDPR or California law) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, or to object to how we use it. Contact us and we will respond.

7. Children

This site and our programs are not directed at anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

8. Third-party links

Our site links to services such as Linktree, WhatsApp, Discord, TikTok and X. We don’t control them and this policy doesn’t cover what they do with your information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.

10. Contact

The best way to reach us is by email at admin@chimeraid.com. You can also message us on WhatsApp or in our Discord server.
